Groq key

The app uses Groq's AI, which is free. Your key is only saved in this browser.

  1. Create an account at console.groq.com (you can sign in with Google).
  2. Go to console.groq.com/keys and tap Create API Key.
  3. Copy the key (it starts with gsk_) and paste it here.

Install on your phone

Whispers gets its own icon and opens full screen, like an app. Your stories stay the same (they live in this browser).

Android (Chrome)

  1. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  2. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  2. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

On iPhone the installed app keeps its own storage, separate from Safari. Use Export backup here and Import in the app to bring your stories over.
